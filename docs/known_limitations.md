# Known limitations and open release gates

- Only 11 cases are included; 6 of 11 are canonical hotspots, and the cohort is not broadly representative.
- The recorded panel has no case within 5 percentage points of the 20% threshold, no case above 140% WT, and no incomplete real-world panel.
- The overall phenotype labels remain provisional; no qualified independent blinded reviewer has completed the worksheet.
- The official bulk R21 file was not obtained or checksummed in the recorded run; 0/88 values are verified against official file bytes. Page-level reconciliation is inherited and was not re-executed in the v1.1.0 validation session.
- Material issues remain unresolved, including the p.V31I overall-label conflict and source-vocabulary differences for annotations.
- Automated verification checks structure and objective fields, but cannot establish the biological correctness of free-text reasoning.
- The task is for research/benchmark evaluation only and must not be used for clinical decision-making.

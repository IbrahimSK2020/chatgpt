# Human review rubric for overall functional phenotype

This rubric is for a qualified independent reviewer. Review the raw evidence without seeing the current overall label or rationale first. The primary yeast panel class is computed mechanically and must not be used as a proxy for clinical pathogenicity.

## Required review fields per case

- Reviewer qualification and review date.
- Confirmed variant identity and transcript/cDNA/protein notation.
- Confirmation of all eight promoter values against the cited source.
- Independent median and primary assay bin.
- Assay model, endpoint, and limitations.
- Whether there is credible evidence for partial function, promoter-specific effects, splice alteration, or conflict among distinct assay systems.
- Overall phenotype, confidence, and supporting or limiting evidence.
- Whether the previous adjudication agrees, disagrees, or cannot be resolved.
- Written reason for any proposed change; no forced consensus.

## Label guidance

- functionally_abnormal: evidence consistently supports substantially impaired function in the specified functional context.
- functionally_preserved: evidence supports retained function in the evaluated contexts, with no major unresolved confound in the evidence packet.
- mixed_or_context_dependent: partial residual activity, promoter-specific heterogeneity, or meaningful assay-context differences prevent a simple global label.
- indeterminate: missing, conflicting, or confounded evidence prevents a defensible overall functional conclusion.

## Prohibited shortcuts

Do not use hotspot status, tumor recurrence, an in-silico score, or a clinical database classification as a substitute for functional evidence. Do not infer gain-of-function from loss of function or DNE. Do not infer clinical pathogenicity from this functional benchmark.
